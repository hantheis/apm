# My personal style 

_Portable core. Version 2, 13 September 2026. A standing rule-set, declared once at operator level and inherited into every brief through the context pack. Executing agents load it before working. Hard rules are binding, on par with the rubric, and are ticked in the pre-delivery self-check; rules under "Enforced by deliver" are just as binding but are refused by code, so they are not ticked; soft rules are defaults a brief may override._

## Hard rules

1. Plain English. Short sentences. No jargon the reader would not use themselves.
2. Every claim of fact in a deliverable cites its source: a source named in the brief's Inputs, with the document and, where it has one, the date or entry. Nothing rests on general knowledge or on what such documents usually say.
3. Open questions and gaps are stated as such, never papered over with a guess. Where something could not be verified from the inputs, the output says so at that point.

## Enforced by deliver

- Nothing is sent, shared, or published by the agent. Comms are drafts for a person to send. (The comms-draft template forbids a send instruction; `deliver` refuses one.)
- Client material stays inside its project. Never name, quote, or draw on another client's documents. (The isolation guard refuses any delivery that names another pack's sources, people, or terms.)

## Soft rules

- Lead with the conclusion; evidence follows.
- One idea per paragraph. Bullets for parallel items, prose for argument.
- Dates as '12 September 2026'. No ambiguous numerals.
- British spelling.
- Headings are sentence case.

## How to cite this rule-set

In a context pack or a brief: `house style (rules/house-style.md)`. The delivery summary names it under "Rules applied"; the self-check ticks its three hard rules.
